Our Heavy-Duty Product Portfolio

Premium HDPE Monofilament Yarn and 100% Virgin Niwar manufactured for export, performance, and long-term industrial use.

HDPE Monofilament Yarn

Engineered for precision weaving, braiding, and knitting applications. Our yarn delivers uniform thickness and high break-load strength.

  • Applications: Fishing nets, agro-shade nets, anti-bird nets, sports nets, ropes, and industrial woven fabrics.
  • Specifications: Available in custom Denier ranges, precise filament counts, and a broad spectrum of color-fast masterbatches.

100% Virgin Niwar (Webbing)

Woven tightly for superior structural hold and aesthetic consistency. Unlike recycled alternatives, our virgin Niwar offers higher load-bearing capabilities.

  • Applications: Traditional and contemporary bed manufacturing, woven outdoor furniture, safety webbing, and cargo lashing.
  • Specifications: Available in multi-colored stripes, custom widths, and specialized roll lengths to match your manufacturing setup.

Export-Ready Infrastructure & Rigorous Quality Assurance

Our production facility features modern extrusion plants and advanced automated looms to deliver flaw-free outputs. Every batch of HDPE Yarn and Virgin Niwar undergoes testing for:

  • Tensile break strength and elongation tests.
  • Constant weight and gauge uniformity checks.
  • Accelerated UV aging performance.

From seaworthy bulk packaging to direct custom port handling logistics, we ensure safe, moisture-protected, and timely delivery to your destination country.
