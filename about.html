About Jay Trading

Industry leaders in polymer extrusion and weaving, delivering consistent HDPE yarn and Virgin Niwar for demanding buyers.

Built for Strength, Finish and Consistency

At Jay Trading, we specialize in manufacturing and exporting high-performance High-Density Polyethylene yarn and Virgin Niwar. Driven by state-of-the-art extrusion machinery and strict quality control protocols, our products cater to agricultural, industrial, furniture, and domestic markets worldwide.

We pride ourselves on consistency. By selecting only the finest grade polymers, we ensure our clients receive materials that endure high tension, resist environmental wear, and maintain long-term structural integrity.

Why Global Buyers Choose Us

Uncompromised Raw Materials

We strictly use 100% virgin polymer to guarantee superior product life, surface finish, and reliable performance.

UV Stabilization

Products can be treated with advanced UV inhibitors for rigorous outdoor applications and long-term exposure.

Bulk Export Capacity

Our infrastructure supports large container-load commitments with seaworthy packaging and export coordination.

Export-Ready Infrastructure

Our production facility features modern extrusion plants and advanced automated looms to deliver flaw-free outputs. Every batch of HDPE Yarn and Virgin Niwar undergoes structured checks before dispatch.

  • Tensile break strength and elongation tests.
  • Constant weight and gauge uniformity checks.
  • Accelerated UV aging performance validation.
